# Lens Assistant Product Demo Video Expansion

## Title + Style Block

Brand baseline comes from the existing Lens Assistant video composition because no frame.md/design.md exists in the repo. Use the current light canvas and brand system: background #f7f9ff / #fbfcff, primary purple #6f5cff, deep ink #080a1f, muted copy #687191, soft panel borders rgba(96,112,166,0.16). Typography remains Avenir Next with heavy display weights and clean Chinese UI text.

## Rhythm Declaration

Rhythm: product-hook -> workflow-build -> preview-peak -> publish-hold. 18 seconds total. The first two beats move quickly to establish real operations, the third beat holds longer on the editor/preview split, and the final beat resolves with platform-ready output.

## Global Rules

- Make this feel like a real app walkthrough, not a generic promo slide.
- Every scene uses product UI panels, buttons, cursors, progress states, and visible before/after changes.
- Keep purple accents visible but avoid repeating the website hero headline.
- Use light UI with strong shadows, 2-3px borders, and large readable Chinese text.
- Transitions are clean UI-style wipes and focus pulls, no loud cinematic effects.

## Scene 1: Pick A Topic, 0-4.2s

Concept: The viewer lands inside the Lens Assistant workbench, not on a marketing slide. A hot-topic list updates, one topic is selected, and the right panel turns that topic into a usable writing angle.

Mood direction: crisp SaaS dashboard, editorial planning room, controlled and useful.

Depth layers: BG soft grid and purple glow; MG app shell with sidebar, hot-topic table, topic detail panel; FG cursor, selected row, live update pill, small progress ticks.

Animation choreography: app shell slides in, hot topics cascade, cursor clicks the first row, selected state locks in, angle chips assemble, CTA button pulses once.

Transition out: vertical UI wipe from selected topic into generation panel, 0.4s power3.inOut.

## Scene 2: Generate Draft, 4.2-8.6s

Concept: The selected topic becomes an article draft. Title suggestions, outline sections, and body paragraphs appear as the generator moves from plan to copy.

Mood direction: mechanical but calm, a visible writing engine.

Depth layers: BG faint timeline; MG generation panel with title, outline, body; FG progress rail, typed lines, status chips.

Animation choreography: progress rail fills, title types on, outline cards step in, paragraphs draw line by line, completion badge snaps on.

Transition out: focus pull into the editor canvas, 0.45s blur crossfade.

## Scene 3: Edit And Preview, 8.6-13.5s

Concept: The draft is no longer a blank artifact; it is editable content with an actual preview. The user switches template, adjusts bottom CTA, and the phone preview updates.

Mood direction: product craft, editorial desk, polished but operational.

Depth layers: BG product chrome; MG split editor and phone preview; FG template pills, CTA field, hover highlight, sync check.

Animation choreography: split layout expands, cursor selects template, preview restyles, CTA field updates, sync check counts in.

Transition out: right-side phone preview slides forward into platform export panel, 0.45s power2.inOut.

## Scene 4: Publish Ready, 13.5-18s

Concept: The content is ready for multiple platforms. It shows WeChat draft, export, and platform adaptation as actual outputs, then ends with a concise brand/action line.

Mood direction: confident finish, launch checklist, ready-to-ship.

Depth layers: BG soft Lens wordmark; MG output cards for 公众号、小红书、知乎; FG success badge, export button, final product mark.

Animation choreography: platform cards stack in, success badge checks, export button glows, final brand lockup rises and holds.

Transition out: subtle fade to final lockup, no extra scene.

## Recurring Motifs

Purple selection rings, rounded app cards, cursor clicks, progress rails, check badges, and soft radial glows.

## Negative Prompt

Avoid repeating “每天10分钟完成一篇爆款内容” inside the video. Avoid pure marketing slides. Avoid fake metrics as the main content. Avoid tiny text, low contrast, or UI panels that cannot be read at homepage size.
